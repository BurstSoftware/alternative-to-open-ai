# how many employees does open ai have

As of mid-2026, OpenAI has roughly 7,500–8,000 employees.

The best recent estimates put it at about 7,450 employees in March 2026, while other reporting says OpenAI had roughly 7,850 at the end of 2025 and was targeting around 8,000 during 2026.

That's a huge increase from just a few years ago. OpenAI had around 770 employees in late 2023, according to reporting.

Roughly what are all those people doing?

They're not all AI researchers. You can think of OpenAI as having several major groups:

🧠 Research & ML engineering — developing and training models
💻 Software engineering — ChatGPT, API, infrastructure, developer products
🖥️ Infrastructure — GPUs, distributed computing, networking, data centers
🛡️ Safety & security — model safety, cybersecurity, evaluations
🎨 Product/design — ChatGPT and other products
💼 Sales & enterprise — selling ChatGPT/API to businesses
⚖️ Legal/policy — regulation, contracts, intellectual property
💰 Finance/operations — money, procurement, HR, recruiting
📣 Marketing/communications
🔬 Research scientists — fundamental AI research

And this creates an interesting contrast with what we were discussing earlier:

~8,000 humans + enormous amounts of automated compute → AI service used by hundreds of millions of people.

OpenAI is also increasingly using its own AI internally. A June 2026 research paper involving OpenAI researchers reported that Codex usage had become nearly universal among OpenAI employees and had substantially changed how employees work.

So the fascinating question is actually how much revenue and infrastructure an ~8,000-person company can generate/manage compared with a traditional tech company with tens or hundreds of thousands of employees.
